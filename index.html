Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Short version: Furtune does not ask for your name, birthday, email, phone number or any account. The cards you draw, your journal and your collection stay on your own device. We never see them. The only data leaving your phone is what Google AdMob needs in order to show ads.

What the app stores

Saved on your device only, never sent anywhere:

  • Today's card, the extra cards you drew and your three-card spreads (the cards and the topic you chose: general, love, work or personal growth)
  • Your tarot journal: which cards you drew on which day
  • Your card collection, HOLO cards and moonstones
  • The date of your last daily gift and your streak
  • Sound effects, music, vibration and reversed cards on/off, and your chosen language

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. There is no server and no backup: if you uninstall, your journal and collection are gone for good.

Motion sensor

HOLO cards shimmer when you tilt your phone. The app reads the motion sensor only while it is open, just to move the shine. These readings are never stored or sent anywhere.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app draws a picture of the card and hands it to your phone's share menu. Nothing is sent unless you pick an app to share with, and the picture goes only where you choose.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial, rewarded and app open ads (an ad that may appear while the app is starting). To do that, Google may collect and process:

  • An advertising identifier and app set ID (resettable IDs, not your name)
  • Your IP address, used to estimate your approximate location
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views
  • Diagnostic and device information, such as device type, operating system version, app launch time and hang rate

Google uses this data to show and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud, and it is sent over encrypted connections.

We configure AdMob with a maximum ad content rating of PG. Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose to watch one to unlock the deep reading for the day, draw an extra card, get extra three-card spreads, open a bonus card pack, or double the daily gift. Google's own privacy policy explains what they do with this data: https://policies.google.com/privacy — you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time under Settings → Google → Ads on your device.

Users in Europe

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app first asks for your consent (using Google's consent form) before your data is used for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. If you do not consent, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

Data security

Furtune has no server and no account, so we never receive or store your data anywhere. Your journal and collection are kept in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. The advertising data described above is collected and sent by Google's AdMob SDK over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and handled under Google's own privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's settings; either one deletes it immediately. We hold no copy that would need deleting.

Age

Furtune is meant for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Entertainment only

Tarot readings in Furtune are for entertainment and self-reflection. They do not predict the future and are not medical, legal, financial or psychological advice.

Permissions

The app requests only the permissions Google AdMob needs to show ads (such as internet access, network state and the advertising ID), plus vibration. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, files or location.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same address with an updated date at the top.
